## 2026-09-16 - Prevent Unnecessary Allocations in Demographic Simulation
**Learning:** The demographic cellular automata simulation was allocating a large number of temporary slices and maps per tile per iteration (specifically the `neighbors` slice and the `scores` map in `spreadFactionInfluence`, and `next` slices in each phase). In a 128x128 map over 8 iterations, this created hundreds of thousands of small allocations, causing the simulation to spend significant time in `mallocgc` and garbage collection.
**Action:** Lift array/slice allocations out of inner loops. Use flat arrays or slice re-use (`targets = targets[:0]`) instead of dynamically allocating slices inside nested loops. Refactor slice-returning methods to accept a `next` output parameter (in-place operation) rather than returning new slices for each simulation iteration. This drops allocations per simulation tick to near-zero and drastically improves execution speed (e.g. from ~70ms to ~15ms per 128x128 simulation).
